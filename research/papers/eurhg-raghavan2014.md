# Raghavan et al. 2014 — Mal'ta (MA-1) genome

- **Citation:** Raghavan M, Skoglund P, Graf KE, et al. (2014) Upper Palaeolithic Siberian genome reveals dual ancestry of Native Americans. *Nature* 505:87–91.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature12736 · PMC4105016 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. MA-1 (Mal'ta, south-central Siberia, ~24 ka) is basal to present-day western Eurasians and closely related to Native Americans, without close affinity to East Asians. *(Abstract.)*
2. An estimated 14–38% of Native American ancestry may derive from gene flow from a population related to MA-1, after divergence from East Asian ancestors and before Native American diversification. *(Abstract.)*
3. Afontova Gora-2 (~17 ka) shows similar autosomal signatures, suggesting the region was occupied through the LGM. *(Abstract.)*

## Notes
- MA-1 and AG-2 are the type individuals for "Ancient North Eurasian (ANE)" ancestry used in later papers.
- The 14–38% range is wide; later studies model Native American ancestry with Ancient Paleo-Siberian / ANE-related contributions differently — record as one model.
