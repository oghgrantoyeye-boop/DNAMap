# Skoglund et al. 2015 — "Population Y"

- **Citation:** Skoglund P, Mallick S, Bortolini MC, et al. (2015) Genetic evidence for two founding populations of the Americas. *Nature* 525:104–108.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature14895 · PMC4982469 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Some Amazonian groups (e.g. Suruí, Karitiana) share a signal of extra affinity to Australasians (Indigenous Australians, New Guineans, Andaman Islanders) not seen (or weaker) in North/Central Americans or Anzick-1, attributed to a founding source the authors call "Population Y". *(Abstract.)*

## Notes
- Present-day data; the ancestral population is a modelled ghost — show as `low`-confidence inferred signal only. Moreno-Mayar 2018b (abstract) reports a related Australasian signal in an ancient individual; Raghavan 2015 also discusses distant Australo-Melanesian sharing.
