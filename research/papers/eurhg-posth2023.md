# Posth et al. 2023 — Upper Palaeolithic to Neolithic European hunter-gatherers

- **Citation:** Posth C, Yu H, Ghalichi A, et al. (2023) Palaeogenomics of Upper Palaeolithic to Neolithic European hunter-gatherers. *Nature* 615:117–126.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-05726-0 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 356 hunter-gatherer genomes (116 new), 35–5 ka, western and central Eurasia. *(Abstract.)*
2. Named genetic clusters: **Fournol** (Gravettian-associated individuals in western Europe; resembles earlier Aurignacian-associated individuals), **Věstonice** (Gravettian-associated individuals in central and southern Europe), **GoyetQ2** (Magdalenian-associated, reappearing after the LGM), **Villabruna** (Epigravettian-associated, southern Europe from ≥17 ka), **Oberkassel** (from ~14 ka; explicitly equated by the authors with "WHG"), **Sidelkino** (the authors' rename of "EHG"). *(Introduction; Results sections; Fig. 2.)*
3. The same archaeological culture (Gravettian) is associated with two genetically distinct clusters — a key example that culture ≠ genetic population. *(Abstract.)*
4. The Fournol-related profile persisted through the LGM (25–19 ka) in southwestern Europe (Solutrean-, then Magdalenian-associated groups). *(Abstract.)*
5. Genetic turnover in southern Europe around the LGM, with Villabruna-cluster ancestry dispersing north-to-south in Italy. *(Abstract; Discussion.)*
6. From ≥14 ka, Villabruna/Oberkassel-related ancestry spread across Europe, largely replacing Magdalenian-associated (GoyetQ2) ancestry. *(Abstract; Discussion point 4.)*
7. Magdalenian-associated individuals modelled as Fournol + Villabruna mixtures: El Mirón ~43% Villabruna, others 19–29%. *(Results; Fig. 4a.)*
8. From ~8 ka, Sidelkino (EHG) ancestry appears in central European hunter-gatherers at ~10%; Oberkassel ancestry reaches the upper Volga by ≥7.5 ka. *(Results; Extended Data Fig. 8.)*

## Notes
- Naming conflict to represent: WHG ≡ Oberkassel cluster ≈ (later part of) Villabruna cluster; EHG ≡ Sidelkino cluster. We use WHG/EHG as primary labels (more widely used) with aliases.
