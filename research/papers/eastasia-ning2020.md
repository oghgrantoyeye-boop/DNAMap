# Ning et al. 2020 — northern China (Yellow, West Liao, Amur rivers)

- **Citation:** Ning C, Li T, Wang K, et al. (2020) Ancient genomes from northern China suggest links between subsistence changes and human migration. *Nature Communications* 11:2700.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-020-16557-2 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 55 genomes, 7500–1700 BP, Yellow River (YR), West Liao River (WLR) and Amur River (AR) regions. *(Abstract.)*
2. Genetic stability in the Amur region; YR and WLR profiles changed over time. YR populations show increasing affinity with present-day southern Chinese/Southeast Asians over time. *(Abstract.)*
3. In the WLR, Late Neolithic farming intensification correlates with increased YR affinity; Bronze Age pastoralism with increased AR affinity. *(Abstract.)*
