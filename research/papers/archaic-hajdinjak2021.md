# Hajdinjak et al. 2021 — Bacho Kiro Cave genomes

- **Citation:** Hajdinjak M, Mafessoni F, Skov L, et al. (2021) Initial Upper Palaeolithic humans in Europe had recent Neanderthal ancestry. *Nature* 592:253–257.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-021-03335-3 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Three individuals from Bacho Kiro Cave (Bulgaria) dated ~45,930–42,580 years ago, associated with an Initial Upper Palaeolithic assemblage. *(Abstract.)*
2. They are more closely related to present-day and ancient East Asians and Native Americans than to later western Eurasians — evidence of a dispersal into Europe not previously seen genetically, with some continuity into later Eurasian populations. *(Abstract.)*
3. All three had Neandertal ancestors a few generations back. *(Abstract.)*
