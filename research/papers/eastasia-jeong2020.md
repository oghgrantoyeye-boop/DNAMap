# Jeong et al. 2020 — 6,000 years of the Eastern Steppe

- **Citation:** Jeong C, Wang K, Wilkin S, et al. (2020) A dynamic 6,000-year genetic history of Eurasia's Eastern Steppe. *Cell* 183:890–904.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cell.2020.10.015 · PMC7664836 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. 214 ancient individuals from Mongolia over 6,000 years. *(Summary.)*
2. A pastoralist expansion into Mongolia ~3000 BCE; by the Late Bronze Age, three geographically structured groups, all practising dairy pastoralism regardless of ancestry. *(Summary.)* — subsistence ≠ ancestry.
3. Xiongnu-period individuals formed by mixing of these and surrounding populations; Mongol-period individuals show much higher eastern Eurasian ancestry. *(Summary.)*
