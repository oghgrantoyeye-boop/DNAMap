# Shinde et al. 2019 — Rakhigarhi (IVC) genome

- **Citation:** Shinde V, Narasimhan VM, Rohland N, et al. (2019) An ancient Harappan genome lacks ancestry from steppe pastoralists or Iranian farmers. *Cell* 179:729–735.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cell.2019.08.048 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. One genome from an IVC context (Rakhigarhi) fits as a mixture of ancient-Iranian-related (largest component) and Southeast Asian hunter-gatherer-related ancestry, matching the 11 Indus Periphery outliers. *(Summary.)*
2. Little or no steppe-pastoralist-derived ancestry, i.e. steppe-related ancestry was not ubiquitous in northwest South Asia during the IVC. *(Summary.)*
3. The Iranian-related ancestry derives from a lineage that split before the ancestors of early Iranian farmers, herders and hunter-gatherers separated — contradicting a large-scale eastward spread of western Iranian farmers. *(Summary.)*
