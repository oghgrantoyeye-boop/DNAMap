# Sikora et al. 2019 — northeastern Siberia since the Pleistocene

- **Citation:** Sikora M, Pitulko VV, Sousa VC, et al. (2019) The population history of northeastern Siberia since the Pleistocene. *Nature* 570:182–188.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-019-1279-z · PMC7617447 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. 34 new genomes, 31,000–600 years ago. *(Abstract.)*
2. Three major events: an initial Palaeolithic "Ancient North Siberian" (ANS) population (Yana RHS, ~31 ka) distantly related to early West Eurasian hunter-gatherers; arrival of East Asian-related people giving rise to Native Americans and "Ancient Paleosiberians"; a Holocene "Neosiberian" migration. *(Abstract.)*
3. Each expansion nearly replaced earlier inhabitants. *(Abstract.)*
