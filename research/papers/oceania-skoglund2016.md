# Skoglund et al. 2016 — peopling of the Southwest Pacific (Lapita-associated individuals)

- **Citation:** Skoglund P, Posth C, Sirak K, et al. (2016) Genomic insights into the peopling of the Southwest Pacific. *Nature* 538:510–513.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature19844 · PMC5515717 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Four individuals from Vanuatu (~3100–2700 BP, Teouma, Lapita-associated) and Tonga (~2700–2300 BP). *(Abstract.)*
2. These earliest Remote Oceanians had little or no Papuan-related ancestry; their East Asian-related ancestry matches the East Asian-origin component in present-day South Pacific peoples, which no longer exists unmixed. *(Abstract.)*
3. Since all present-day groups in the region carry ≥25% Papuan-related ancestry, Papuan-related ancestry must have spread through the South Pacific after first settlement. *(Abstract.)*
