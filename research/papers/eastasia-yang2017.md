# Yang et al. 2017 — Tianyuan (40 ka, China)

- **Citation:** Yang MA, Gao X, Theunert C, et al. (2017) 40,000-year-old individual from Asia provides insight into early population structure in Eurasia. *Current Biology* 27:3202–3208.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cub.2017.09.030 · PMC6592271 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Tianyuan (~40 ka, near Beijing) is more related to present-day and ancient Asians than to Europeans. *(Summary.)*
2. He shares more alleles with GoyetQ116-1 (~35 ka, Belgium) than with other ancient Europeans: the separation of early European and early Asian populations was not a single clean split. *(Summary.)*
3. He shares more alleles with some South American groups than with other Native Americans, suggesting Asian population structure persisting from 40 ka to the peopling of the Americas. *(Summary.)*
