# Mathieson et al. 2018 — genomic history of southeastern Europe

- **Citation:** Mathieson I, Alpaslan-Roodenberg S, Posth C, et al. (2018) The genomic history of southeastern Europe. *Nature* 555:197–203.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature25778 · PMC6091220 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. 225 individuals from southeastern Europe and surroundings, 12,000–500 BCE. *(Abstract.)*
2. Farming reached Europe in the mid-7th millennium BCE, associated with migrants from Anatolia settling first in the southeast. *(Abstract, as framing.)*
3. A west–east cline of ancestry among hunter-gatherers (WHG-like to EHG-like). *(Abstract.)*
4. The first farmers of northern and western Europe passed through southeastern Europe with limited hunter-gatherer admixture; some groups that stayed mixed extensively, without the male-biased hunter-gatherer admixture seen later in the north and west. *(Abstract.)*
5. Intermittent contact with the steppe up to ~2,000 years before the major steppe-related migrations. *(Abstract.)*
