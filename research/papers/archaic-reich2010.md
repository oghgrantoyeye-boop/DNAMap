# Reich et al. 2010 — Denisova Cave genome

- **Citation:** Reich D, Green RE, Kircher M, et al. (2010) Genetic history of an archaic hominin group from Denisova Cave in Siberia. *Nature* 468:1053–1060.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature09710 · PMC4306417 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. A finger bone from Denisova Cave (southern Siberia) yielded a genome from an archaic group sharing a common origin with Neandertals; the authors name this group "Denisovans". *(Abstract.)*
2. The Denisovan group was not the source of the Neandertal-related gene flow seen in all non-Africans. *(Abstract.)*
3. Denisovan-related ancestry is estimated at 4–6% of the genomes of present-day Melanesians (first estimate). *(Abstract; section "Denisovan gene flow into the ancestors of Melanesians".)*

## Notes
- The Denisovan contributor to Melanesians was not the sequenced Altai individual's population itself but a related group; keep source labelled as "Denisovan-related".
