# Nakatsuka et al. 2020 — ContamLD: nuclear contamination from linkage disequilibrium

- **Citation:** Nakatsuka N, Harney É, Mallick S, Mah M, Patterson N, Reich D. (2020) ContamLD: estimation of ancient nuclear DNA contamination using breakdown of linkage disequilibrium. *Genome Biology* 21:199.
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s13059-020-02111-2 · PMC7418405 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Contamination can enter ancient DNA data during excavation and storage of skeletal material and during laboratory processing. *(Background.)* [ev-nakatsuka2020contam-1]
2. Mitochondrial DNA sequences are almost always uniform within an uncontaminated individual, which is the basis of mitochondrial contamination tests; but such estimates can be biased because the ratio of mitochondrial to nuclear DNA varies by orders of magnitude between samples; estimates from apparent variation on the X chromosome of males are more consistently reliable but do not work for females. *(Background.)* [ev-nakatsuka2020contam-2]
3. ContamLD estimates contamination on the autosomes from the breakdown of linkage disequilibrium (correlations between nearby variants) that contaminating DNA causes; it works for males and females, and the authors recommend removing, or explicitly modelling, samples estimated above 5% contamination. *(Abstract; Background; Discussion and conclusion.)* [ev-nakatsuka2020contam-3]
4. Keeping only damaged sequences discards most authentic sequences, and cannot separate authentic DNA from contamination by another ancient individual whose DNA is also damaged, for example through cross-contamination in the laboratory. *(Background.)* [ev-nakatsuka2020contam-4]
