# Renaud et al. 2015 — schmutzi: mitochondrial contamination estimates

- **Citation:** Renaud G, Slon V, Duggan AT, Kelso J. (2015) Schmutzi: estimation of contamination and endogenous mitochondrial consensus calling for ancient DNA. *Genome Biology* 16:224.
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s13059-015-0776-0 · PMC4601135 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Microbial DNA often makes up the bulk of DNA extracted from ancient human remains; bacterial sequences usually do not align to the human reference genome, but contaminating DNA from present-day people does, alongside the endogenous fragments, and can distort consensus calls and genotypes. *(Introduction.)* [ev-renaud2015-1]
2. Their method uses deamination patterns and fragment lengths to estimate present-day human contamination in mitochondrial data and to reconstruct the ancient individual's own mitochondrial genome. *(Abstract.)* [ev-renaud2015-2]
