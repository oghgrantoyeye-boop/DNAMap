# Lazaridis et al. 2025 — genetic origin of the Yamnaya

- **Citation:** Lazaridis I, Patterson N, Anthony D, et al. (2025) The genetic origin of the Indo-Europeans. *Nature* 639:132–142.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-024-08531-5 · PMC11922553 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 428 individuals; the Yamnaya complex appeared ~3300 BCE north of the Black and Caspian Seas and by ~3000 BCE extended from Hungary to Kazakhstan. *(Abstract.)*
2. Three genetic clines among preceding Eneolithic people: a **Caucasus–Lower Volga (CLV)** cline rich in CHG-related ancestry; a **Volga** cline (CLV × EHG, e.g. Khvalynsk); a **Dnipro** cline (CLV × Ukraine Neolithic hunter-gatherers, e.g. Serednii Stih). *(Abstract.)*
3. Yamnaya ancestors formed ~4000 BCE from Serednii Stih-related groups and expanded rapidly after 3750–3350 BCE; CLV-related people contributed ~four-fifths of Yamnaya ancestry. *(Abstract.)*
4. Don Yamnaya modelled as 79.4 ± 1.1% "Core Yamnaya" + 20.6 ± 1.1% Ukraine Neolithic hunter-gatherer (UNHG). *(Results.)*
5. CLV-related ancestry contributed at least a tenth of the ancestry of Bronze Age central Anatolians. *(Abstract.)*

## Notes
- The language-homeland proposal (Proto-Indo-Anatolian among CLV people 4400–4000 BCE) is not displayed.
- Competing models of Yamnaya formation to store side by side: Haak 2015 (EHG + Near Eastern-related), Jones 2015/Lazaridis 2016 (EHG + CHG/Iran-related), Allentoft 2024 (Middle Don HG contribution), this paper (CLV + UNHG via Serednii Stih).
