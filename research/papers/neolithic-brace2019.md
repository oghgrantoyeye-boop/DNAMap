# Brace et al. 2019 — Early Neolithic Britain

- **Citation:** Brace S, Diekmann Y, Booth TJ, et al. (2019) Ancient genomes indicate population replacement in Early Neolithic Britain. *Nature Ecology & Evolution* 3:765–771.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41559-019-0871-9 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Six Mesolithic and 67 Neolithic individuals from Britain, 8500–2500 BCE. *(Abstract.)*
2. Farming was introduced by incoming continental farmers; British Neolithic individuals carry mostly Anatolian-farmer-related ancestry (>56%, ~74% on average in their model) with small, geographically structured hunter-gatherer ancestry and no later hunter-gatherer resurgence. *(Abstract; Results.)*
3. Affinities to Iberian Neolithic individuals suggest descent mainly from farmers who followed the Mediterranean route. *(Abstract.)*
4. Modelled arrival of continental farmers by 3975–3722 cal BCE (95%). *(Results.)*
