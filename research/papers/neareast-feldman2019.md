# Feldman et al. 2019 — Pınarbaşı hunter-gatherer and early Anatolian farmers

- **Citation:** Feldman M, Fernández-Domínguez E, Reynolds L, et al. (2019) Late Pleistocene human genome suggests a local origin for the first farmers of central Anatolia. *Nature Communications* 10:1218.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-019-09209-7 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. A ~15,000-year-old Anatolian hunter-gatherer (Pınarbaşı) and early farmers from Anatolia and the Levant. *(Abstract.)*
2. High genetic continuity (~80–90%) between central Anatolian hunter-gatherers and early farmers there, with two additional incoming ancestries (Iranian/Caucasus-related earlier, Levant-related later). *(Abstract.)*
3. Suggests a limited role of migration in the emergence of farming in central Anatolia. *(Abstract.)*
4. A genetic link between southern Europe and the Near East older than 15 ka. *(Abstract.)*
