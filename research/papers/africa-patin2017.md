# Patin et al. 2017 — dispersals of Bantu-speaking populations

- **Citation:** Patin E, Lopez M, Grollemund R, et al. (2017) Dispersals and genetic adaptation of Bantu-speaking populations in Africa and North America. *Science* 356:543–546.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aal1988 · **Verification:** abstract_only (present-day genomes)

## Claims we use (abstract only)
1. Early Bantu speakers first moved south through the equatorial rainforest before spreading toward eastern and southern Africa (inferred from 1,318 present-day individuals, 35 populations).
