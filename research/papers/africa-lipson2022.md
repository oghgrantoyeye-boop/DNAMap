# Lipson et al. 2022 — deep structure in sub-Saharan African foragers

- **Citation:** Lipson M, Sawchuk EA, Thompson JC, et al. (2022) Ancient DNA and deep population structure in sub-Saharan African foragers. *Nature* 603:290–296.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-022-04430-9 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Six new individuals from eastern and south-central Africa spanning ~18,000 years, plus reanalysis of 28 others. *(Abstract.)*
2. Ancestry modelled as a geographically structured mixture of three highly divergent sources (deep eastern African, deep southern African, and a widespread component highest today in central African rainforest hunter-gatherers), probably reflecting interactions ~80–20 ka. *(Abstract.)*
3. Once established, this structure was stable, with limited long-range gene flow — increasing regionalization at the end of the Pleistocene. *(Abstract.)*
