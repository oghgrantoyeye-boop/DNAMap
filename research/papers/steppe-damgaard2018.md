# de Barros Damgaard et al. 2018 — first horse herders

- **Citation:** de Barros Damgaard P, Martiniano R, Kamm J, et al. (2018) The first horse herders and the impact of early Bronze Age steppe expansions into Asia. *Science* 360:eaar7711.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aar7711 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. Botai-associated people (earliest evidence of horse husbandry) derived from a hunter-gatherer population deeply diverged from the Yamnaya. *(Abstract.)*
2. No evidence of steppe-related ancestry in Bronze Age Anatolia. *(Abstract.)*
3. Early Bronze Age Yamnaya-related migrations had limited direct genetic impact in Asia, in contrast to Europe. *(Abstract.)*
