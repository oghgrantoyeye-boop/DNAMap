# Wang et al. 2020 — food production across sub-Saharan Africa

- **Citation:** Wang K, Goldstein S, Bleasdale M, et al. (2020) Ancient genomes reveal complex patterns of population movement, interaction, and replacement in sub-Saharan Africa. *Science Advances* 6:eaaz0183.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/sciadv.aaz0183 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. Kenyan individuals 3900–1500 BP form a forager cluster and a pastoralist cluster. *(Results, "New aDNA from Africa".)*
2. Pastoralist individuals carry variable Dinka-related and eastern-African-forager-related ancestry (e.g. Hyrax Hill 2300 BP 33 ± 11% Dinka-related), with northeastern African/Levantine-related ancestry around 30–40%. *(Results, "Complex spread of pastoralism".)*
3. Iron Age shifts in central/eastern Africa and direct evidence of exchange between Bantu-speaker-associated and pastoralist/forager groups in southern Africa. *(Section headings / Results.)*
