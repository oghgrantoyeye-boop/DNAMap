# Gansauge et al. 2017 — single-stranded library preparation with T4 DNA ligase

- **Citation:** Gansauge MT, Gerber T, Glocke I, et al. (2017) Single-stranded DNA library preparation from highly degraded DNA using T4 DNA ligase. *Nucleic Acids Research* 45:e79.
- **DOI:** 10.1093/nar/gkx033 · PMC5449542 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Library preparation attaches synthetic adapters to the ends of DNA molecules, which allows them to be amplified and primes the sequencing reaction; current machines sequence millions or billions of fragments in parallel. *(Introduction.)* [ev-gansauge2017-1]
2. Single-stranded methods convert each strand of a fragment into a library molecule separately, doubling the chance that at least one strand is recovered and making molecules with single-strand breaks usable. *(Introduction; Fig 1.)* [ev-gansauge2017-2]
3. Compared with the best double-stranded method, single-stranded preparation gave on average 11.6 times more informative sequence from ancient DNA extracts (about an order of magnitude) and a higher share of mapped sequences. *(Results: A comparison of library preparation methods; Fig 4; Discussion.)* [ev-gansauge2017-3]
4. In a widely used double-stranded method, about half of the molecules end up with identical adapters and cannot be amplified or sequenced, yet the method is robust and inexpensive; the Illumina-type method needs a size-selective clean-up and lost short molecules. *(Results: A comparison of library preparation methods.)* [ev-gansauge2017-4]
5. Single-stranded preparation remains more time-consuming than double-stranded methods, and many ancient DNA studies still use the cheaper, simpler double-stranded methods. *(Introduction; Discussion.)* [ev-gansauge2017-5]
6. Most sequences in their ancient bone libraries were microbial; the authors describe microbial DNA as the dominant source of DNA in most ancient bones. *(Fig 3 legend.)* [ev-gansauge2017-6]
7. DNA was extracted from 50–68 mg of bone powder using the Dabney et al. silica method. *(Materials and Methods: DNA extraction.)* [ev-gansauge2017-7]

## Notes
- Bones tested were animal bones; comparisons are by the method's developers.
