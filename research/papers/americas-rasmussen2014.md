# Rasmussen et al. 2014 — Anzick-1 (Clovis-associated)

- **Citation:** Rasmussen M, Anzick SL, Waters MR, et al. (2014) The genome of a Late Pleistocene human from a Clovis burial site in western Montana. *Nature* 506:225–229.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature13025 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Anzick-1: infant, 10,705 ± 35 14C BP (~12,707–12,556 cal BP), directly associated with Clovis tools. *(Abstract.)*
2. More closely related to all Indigenous American populations than to any other group; ANE (Mal'ta)-related gene flow into Native American ancestors is shared by Anzick-1, so it happened before ~12.6 ka. *(Abstract.)*
3. Evidence for a deep divergence among Native American populations predating Anzick-1. *(Abstract.)*

## Notes
- Data generated with consultation of Native American communities; reburial occurred. Display respectfully; no images of remains.
