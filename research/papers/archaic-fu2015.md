# Fu et al. 2015 — Oase 1

- **Citation:** Fu Q, Hajdinjak M, Moldovan OT, et al. (2015) An early modern human from Romania with a recent Neanderthal ancestor. *Nature* 524:216–219.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature14558 · PMC4537386 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Oase 1 (Peştera cu Oase, Romania, ~37–42 ka) has ~6–9% Neandertal-derived DNA, with segments long enough to imply a Neandertal ancestor 4–6 generations back. *(Abstract.)*
2. Oase 1 does not share more alleles with later Europeans than with East Asians — no detectable contribution to later Europeans. *(Abstract.)*
