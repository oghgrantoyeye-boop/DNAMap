# Prendergast et al. 2019 — spread of herding into eastern Africa

- **Citation:** Prendergast ME, Lipson M, Sawchuk EA, et al. (2019) Ancient DNA reveals a multistep spread of the first herders into sub-Saharan Africa. *Science* 365:eaaw6275.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aaw6275 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. 41 individuals from Later Stone Age, Pastoral Neolithic (PN) and Iron Age contexts in Kenya and Tanzania. *(Abstract.)*
2. Multi-phase model: admixture between northeastern-African-related peoples and eastern African foragers formed pastoralist groups, including a homogeneous PN cluster; further northeastern- and western-African-related admixture by the Iron Age. *(Abstract.)*
3. No genetic differentiation between makers of distinct PN artefact traditions (Savanna PN vs Elmenteitan). *(Abstract.)* — culture ≠ population.
