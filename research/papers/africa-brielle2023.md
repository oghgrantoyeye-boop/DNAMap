# Brielle et al. 2023 — medieval Swahili coast

- **Citation:** Brielle ES, Fleisher J, Wynne-Jones S, et al. (2023) Entwined African and Asian genetic roots of medieval peoples of the Swahili coast. *Nature* 615:866–873.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-05754-w · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 80 individuals from six medieval/early modern coastal towns (1250–1800 CE) and one inland town (>1650 CE). *(Abstract.)*
2. Many coastal individuals have more than half their DNA from African ancestors (primarily female), and a large share — sometimes more than half — from Asian ancestors, of which 80–90% traces to Persian-related men. *(Abstract.)* — sex-biased admixture.
3. African and Asian ancestries began to mix by ~1000 CE; before ~1500 CE the Southwest Asian ancestry was mainly Persian-related, later increasingly Arabian-related. *(Abstract.)*
