# Raghavan et al. 2015 — Pleistocene and recent history of Native Americans

- **Citation:** Raghavan M, Steinrücken M, Harris K, et al. (2015) Genomic evidence for the Pleistocene and recent population history of Native Americans. *Science* 349:aab3884.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aab3884 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. Ancestors of all present-day Native Americans entered the Americas from Siberia in a single migration wave no earlier than 23 ka, after at most ~8,000 years of isolation in Beringia. *(Abstract.)*
2. Diversification into two basal branches around 13 ka (one across North and South America, one restricted to North America). *(Abstract.)*
3. Putative "Paleoamerican" relict groups (Pericúes, Fuego-Patagonians) are not directly related to Australo-Melanesians. *(Abstract.)*
