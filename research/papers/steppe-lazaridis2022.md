# Lazaridis et al. 2022 — genetic history of the Southern Arc

- **Citation:** Lazaridis I, Alpaslan-Roodenberg S, Acar A, et al. (2022) The genetic history of the Southern Arc: a bridge between West Asia and Europe. *Science* 377:eabm4247.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.abm4247 · PMC10064553 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. 727 ancient individuals from Anatolia, southeastern Europe and West Asia over ~10,000 years. *(Abstract.)*
2. Gene flow carried Caucasus- and Anatolian/Levantine-related ancestry northward to the steppe; Yamnaya-associated people later spread south into the Balkans and across the Caucasus into Armenia (with many patrilineal descendants there). *(Abstract.)*
3. Bronze Age Anatolia was shaped by gene flow within West Asia, with negligible impact from Yamnaya-related migrations. *(Abstract.)*

## Notes
- The paper's language-homeland proposal (Indo-Anatolian homeland in West Asia) is a linguistic interpretation — not displayed. The genetic claim (little Yamnaya-related ancestry in Bronze Age Anatolia) agrees with de Barros Damgaard 2018.
