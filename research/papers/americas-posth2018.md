# Posth et al. 2018 — deep history of Central and South America

- **Citation:** Posth C, Nakatsuka N, Lazaridis I, et al. (2018) Reconstructing the deep population history of Central and South America. *Cell* 175:1185–1197.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cell.2018.10.027 · PMC6327247 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. 49 individuals in four time transects (Belize, Brazil, Central Andes, Southern Cone), each starting ≥~9 ka. *(Summary.)*
2. The common ancestral population radiated rapidly from one of the two early Native American branches. *(Summary.)*
3. Two streams of gene flow between North and South America: one reaching the Central Andes by ~4,200 years ago; another linking Anzick-1 (Clovis-associated) to the oldest individuals from Chile, Brazil and Belize. *(Summary.)*
4. Later South Americans derive from lineages without specific Clovis affinity — a population replacement beginning ≥9 ka, followed by substantial regional continuity. *(Summary.)*
