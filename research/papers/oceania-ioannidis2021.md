# Ioannidis et al. 2021 — peopling of Polynesia from genomic networks

- **Citation:** Ioannidis AG, Blanco-Portillo J, Sandoval K, et al. (2021) Paths and timings of the peopling of Polynesia inferred from genomic networks. *Nature* 597:522–526.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-021-03902-8 · **Verification:** full_text (present-day genomes)

## Claims we use
1. From present-day genomes (430 individuals, 21 island populations), a serial-founder settlement sequence originating in Samoa, through Rarotonga, to the Society Islands (~11th century), western Austral Islands and Tuāmotu (~12th century), and then Marquesas, Raivavae and Rapa Nui (~1200 CE, via Mangareva). *(Abstract.)*

## Notes
- Inferred from present-day data with model-based dates; `medium` confidence. Archaeological date disagreements (noted by the authors) exist; do not draw routes — show island-group appearances as dated, with the sequence as an inferred relationship.
