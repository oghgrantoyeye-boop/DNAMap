# Moreno-Mayar et al. 2018b — early dispersals within the Americas

- **Citation:** Moreno-Mayar JV, Vinner L, de Barros Damgaard P, et al. (2018) Early human dispersals within the Americas. *Science* 362:eaav2621.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aav2621 · **Verification:** abstract_only

## Claims we use (abstract only)
1. 15 ancient genomes from Alaska to Patagonia (six ≥10 ka): all most closely related to Native Americans, including morphologically distinct "Paleoamericans".
2. Rapid dispersal and early diversification; one migration carries a Late Pleistocene Australasian-related signal; a later Mesoamerican-related expansion.
