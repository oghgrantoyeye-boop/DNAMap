# Cooke et al. 2021 — tripartite origins of Japanese populations

- **Citation:** Cooke NP, Mattiangeli V, Cassidy LM, et al. (2021) Ancient genomics reveals tripartite origins of Japanese populations. *Science Advances* 7:eabh2419.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/sciadv.abh2419 · PMC8448447 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. Jōmon-related ancestry, then Northeast Asian-related ancestry in the Yayoi period, then widespread East Asian-related (Han-like) ancestry in the Kofun period: three ancestral components. *(Main text, Discussion.)*
2. Yayoi individuals can alternatively be fit as Jōmon + a Late Bronze/Iron Age Yellow River population (itself ~40% Northeast Asian / ~60% East Asian) — an example of proxy-dependent models. *(Results.)*
