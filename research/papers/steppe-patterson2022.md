# Patterson et al. 2022 — Middle–Late Bronze Age migration into Britain

- **Citation:** Patterson N, Isakov M, Booth T, et al. (2022) Large-scale migration into Britain during the Middle to Late Bronze Age. *Nature* 601:588–594.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-021-04287-4 · PMC8889665 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 793 new individuals. Between 1000–875 BCE, EEF-related ancestry increased in southern Britain (England and Wales) but not Scotland, through migrants arriving then and over previous centuries, genetically most similar to ancient individuals from France. *(Abstract.)*
2. These migrants contributed about half the ancestry of Iron Age people of England and Wales. *(Abstract.)*
3. Lactase persistence allele at ~50% in Iron Age Britain vs ~7% in central Europe. *(Abstract.)*
