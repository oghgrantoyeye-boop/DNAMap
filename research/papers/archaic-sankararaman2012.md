# Sankararaman et al. 2012 — dating Neandertal gene flow

- **Citation:** Sankararaman S, Patterson N, Li H, Pääbo S, Reich D. (2012) The date of interbreeding between Neandertals and modern humans. *PLoS Genetics* 8:e1002947.
- **DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pgen.1002947 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. Using linkage disequilibrium in present-day Europeans, the last Neandertal (or related) gene flow into their ancestors is dated to 37,000–86,000 years BP, most likely 47,000–65,000 years ago. *(Abstract; Results.)*
2. This date supports recent interbreeding after modern humans left Africa rather than ancient African substructure. *(Abstract.)*
3. Genetics alone does not locate the encounter; the authors suggest western Eurasia (possibly the Middle East) on archaeological grounds — **this location is a hypothesis, not a result; we do not map it.** *(Discussion.)*
