# Nakatsuka et al. 2020 — deep history of the Andes

- **Citation:** Nakatsuka N, Lazaridis I, Barbieri C, et al. (2020) A paleogenomic reconstruction of the deep population history of the Andes. *Cell* 181:1131–1145.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cell.2020.04.015 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. 89 individuals, ~9,000–500 BP. Present-day Andean genetic structure began forming by ~5,800 BP. *(Summary.)*
2. Minimal admixture among neighbouring groups ~2,000–500 BP, though cosmopolitan (mixed-ancestry) communities in Tiwanaku and Inca heartlands. *(Summary.)*
