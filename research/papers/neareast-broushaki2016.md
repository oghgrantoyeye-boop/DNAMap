# Broushaki et al. 2016 — Early Neolithic genomes from the Zagros

- **Citation:** Broushaki F, Thomas MG, Link V, et al. (2016) Early Neolithic genomes from the eastern Fertile Crescent. *Science* 353:499–503.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aaf7943 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. Early Neolithic individuals from the Zagros (Iran) represent a population that is neither ancestral to the first European farmers nor a significant contributor to present-day Europeans. *(Abstract.)*
2. Estimated separation from Anatolian Early Neolithic farmers ~46–77 ka (model-based). *(Abstract.)*
3. Multiple genetically differentiated hunter-gatherer populations adopted farming in southwest Asia. *(Abstract.)*
