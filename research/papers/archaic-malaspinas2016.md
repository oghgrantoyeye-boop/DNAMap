# Malaspinas et al. 2016 — Aboriginal Australian genomes

- **Citation:** Malaspinas AS, Westaway MC, Muller C, et al. (2016) A genomic history of Aboriginal Australia. *Nature* 538:207–214.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature18299 · PMC7617037 · **Verification:** full_text (present-day genomes)

## Claims we use
1. Site-frequency-spectrum modelling gives a primary Neandertal admixture of ~2.3% (95% CI 1.1–3.5%) into the common ancestors of all non-Africans at ~60 ka (95% CI 55–84 ka). *(Main text "Archaic admixture"; S07.)*
2. Aboriginal Australians and Papuans carry more Denisovan-derived haplotypes than other non-Africans. *(Same section; Extended Data Fig. 3.)*
3. When archaic introgression is modelled explicitly, the data favour a single out-of-Africa origin for Australo-Papuans and Eurasians (a model without archaic introgression suggested two waves). *(Section "Out of Africa".)*

## Notes
- Present-day genomes, but the inferences concern events inside V1's date range. The Australian data come from community consultation; any display must respect that context.
