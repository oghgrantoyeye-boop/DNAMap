# Bennett et al. 2021 — White Sands footprints

- **Citation:** Bennett MR, Bustos D, Pigati JS, et al. (2021) Evidence of humans in North America during the Last Glacial Maximum. *Science* 373:1528–1531.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.abg7586 · **Verification:** abstract_only

## Claims we use (abstract only)
1. Human footprints at White Sands (New Mexico) bracketed by seed layers with calibrated radiocarbon ages ~23–21 ka.

## Notes
- Archaeological, not genetic, evidence. The dating has been debated in the literature (we have not yet read the critiques or follow-up dating; logged in LOG.md as an open item). Show as `medium`-confidence archaeological context, and show the tension with genetic models that place the main Native American diversification later (~17.5–14.6 ka) — these are not necessarily contradictory.
