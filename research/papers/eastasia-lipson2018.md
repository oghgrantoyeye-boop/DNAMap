# Lipson et al. 2018 (Science) — Southeast Asian prehistory

- **Citation:** Lipson M, Cheronet O, Mallick S, et al. (2018) Ancient genomes document multiple waves of migration in Southeast Asian prehistory. *Science* 361:92–95.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aat3188 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. 18 individuals, Neolithic to Iron Age (4100–1700 BP). *(Abstract.)*
2. Early farmers at Man Bac (Vietnam) show a mixture of East Asian (southern Chinese agriculturalist-related) and deeply diverged eastern Eurasian hunter-gatherer ancestry. *(Abstract.)*
3. By the Bronze Age, sites in Vietnam and Myanmar show additional influxes of migrants. *(Abstract.)*
