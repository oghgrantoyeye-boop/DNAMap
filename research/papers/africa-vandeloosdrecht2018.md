# van de Loosdrecht et al. 2018 — Taforalt (Morocco)

- **Citation:** van de Loosdrecht M, Bouzouggar A, Humphrey L, et al. (2018) Pleistocene North African genomes link Near Eastern and sub-Saharan African human populations. *Science* 360:548–552.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aar8380 · **Verification:** abstract_only

## Claims we use (abstract only)
1. Seven ~15,000-year-old individuals (Iberomaurusian-associated) from Taforalt, Morocco.
2. Genetic affinity with early Holocene Near Easterners, best represented by Natufians; no evidence of gene flow from Palaeolithic Europeans.
3. About one-third of their ancestry is sub-Saharan African-related, best approximated by a mix of components found in present-day West and East Africans.
