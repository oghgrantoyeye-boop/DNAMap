# Olalde et al. 2019 — genomic history of the Iberian Peninsula

- **Citation:** Olalde I, Mallick S, Patterson N, et al. (2019) The genomic history of the Iberian Peninsula over the past 8000 years. *Science* 363:1230–1234.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aav4040 · PMC6436108 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. 271 ancient Iberians. Strong substructure between northwestern and southeastern hunter-gatherers before farming. *(Abstract.)*
2. Sporadic contacts with North Africa by ~2500 BCE. *(Abstract.)*
3. By ~2000 BCE, ~40% of Iberia's ancestry and nearly 100% of Y chromosomes were replaced by people carrying steppe-related ancestry — a strongly male-biased process. *(Abstract.)*
4. Iron Age: steppe-related ancestry present in both Indo-European- and non-Indo-European-speaking regions. *(Abstract.)*
5. From at least the Roman period, gene flow from North Africa and the eastern Mediterranean. *(Abstract.)*
