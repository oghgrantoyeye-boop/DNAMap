# Nikitin et al. 2025 — North Pontic genomic history

- **Citation:** Nikitin AG, Lazaridis I, Patterson N, et al. (2025) A genomic history of the North Pontic Region from the Neolithic to the Bronze Age. *Nature* 639:124–131.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-024-08372-2 · PMC11909631 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 81 prehistoric North Pontic individuals. *(Abstract.)*
2. North Pontic foragers carried Balkan- and EHG-related ancestry plus European-farmer-related and occasional CHG-related ancestry. *(Abstract.)*
3. Usatove-associated people (~4500 BCE) formed as CLV-related migrants mixed in roughly equal parts with Trypillia-associated farmers. *(Abstract.)*
4. A genetically Yamnaya-like individual from Mykhailivka (3635–3383 BCE) bridges Serednii Stih and Yamnaya. *(Abstract.)*
