# Moreno-Mayar et al. 2018a — Upward Sun River (Ancient Beringians)

- **Citation:** Moreno-Mayar JV, Potter BA, Vinner L, et al. (2018) Terminal Pleistocene Alaskan genome reveals first founding population of Native Americans. *Nature* 553:203–207.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature25173 · **Verification:** abstract_only

## Claims we use (abstract only)
1. USR1 (Upward Sun River, Alaska, ~11.5 ka) is closest to Native Americans but basal to all of them — a distinct "Ancient Beringian" population.
2. Demographic model: the founding population split from East Asians ~36 ± 1.5 ka with gene flow until ~25 ± 1.1 ka; ANE-related gene flow into all Native Americans 25–20 ka; Ancient Beringians branched ~22–18.1 ka; northern and southern Native American branches diverged ~17.5–14.6 ka, probably south of the ice sheets.
3. Consistent with a Beringian "standstill" model.
