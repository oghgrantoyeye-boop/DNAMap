# Lipson et al. 2020 — Shum Laka (Cameroon)

- **Citation:** Lipson M, Ribot I, Mallick S, et al. (2020) Ancient West African foragers in the context of African population history. *Nature* 577:665–670.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-020-1929-1 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Four children buried ~8000 and ~3000 years ago at Shum Laka (Cameroon), within the probable homeland of Bantu languages. *(Abstract.)*
2. Their ancestry is most similar to west-central African hunter-gatherers; present-day western Cameroonians and Bantu speakers across Africa are not substantially descended from this population. *(Abstract.)*
3. An Africa-wide model with widespread admixture and three prominent radiations, one giving rise to at least four major lineages deep in modern human history. *(Abstract.)*
