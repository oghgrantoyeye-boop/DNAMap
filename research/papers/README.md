# research/papers/

One note per paper we rely on. Each note lists only the claims this project uses, **in our own words**, with where in the paper the claim is supported (section, figure, table, supplement). We do not copy text or figures.

**Verification level** (recorded per note and carried into `Evidence.verification`):
- `full_text` — we read the relevant passage in the full text (open-access or PMC author manuscript).
- `abstract_only` — only the abstract was accessible; claims from such papers are limited to what the abstract states, and get at most `medium` confidence unless corroborated by a full-text source.

Notes are grouped by filename prefix: `archaic-`, `ooa-` (early dispersals), `eur-hg-`, `neareast-`, `neolithic-`, `steppe-`, `southasia-`, `eastasia-`, `americas-`, `oceania-`, `africa-`, `data-`.

Key: `AuthorYear` in the filename matches the `source_id` used in `data/curated/sources.json`.
