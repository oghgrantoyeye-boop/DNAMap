# Lipson et al. 2017 — parallel transects of early European farmers

- **Citation:** Lipson M, Szécsényi-Nagy A, Mallick S, et al. (2017) Parallel palaeogenomic transects reveal complex genetic history of early European farmers. *Nature* 551:368–372.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature24476 · **Verification:** full_text (PMC author manuscript)

## Claims we use
1. 180 individuals from Hungary (6000–2900 BCE), Germany (5500–3000 BCE) and Spain (5500–2200 BCE). *(Abstract.)*
2. Hunter-gatherer ancestry in farmers varied regionally and increased over time; admixture was pervasive and mostly local. *(Abstract.)*
3. Examples: LBK (Germany) ~4–5% hunter-gatherer ancestry, average admixture date 5545 ± 65 BCE; Germany Middle Neolithic ~17%; Iberia Middle Neolithic ~23%, Chalcolithic ~27%. *(Main text, regional sections.)*
4. At Blätterhöhle, farmer individuals carried 40–50% hunter-gatherer ancestry. *(Main text.)*
