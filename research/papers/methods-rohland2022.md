# Rohland et al. 2022 — three assays for in-solution enrichment at a million SNPs

- **Citation:** Rohland N, Mallick S, Mah M, Maier R, Patterson N, Reich D. (2022) Three assays for in-solution enrichment of ancient human DNA at more than a million SNPs. *Genome Research* 32:2068–2078.
- **DOI:** 10.1101/gr.276728.122 · PMC9808625 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Of more than 3,000 ancient individuals for whom the authors' laboratory had published genome-wide data by the end of 2021, about half had less than 10% human DNA (best library per individual). *(Introduction; Methods: Estimated fraction of published ancient human genomes with <10% endogenous DNA.)* [ev-rohland2022-1]
2. In in-solution enrichment, synthetic oligonucleotide baits pair with complementary target molecules in a library, so other molecules can be washed away and the bound ones isolated and sequenced. *(Introduction.)* [ev-rohland2022-2]
3. Worked example: at about 600,000 SNPs, roughly 1 in 100 human sequences overlaps a target; in a library that is 1% human, about 1 in 10,000 sequences is informative, so 400 million shotgun reads give at most about 40,000 SNPs, whereas 25 million reads after enrichment can cover nearly all targets several times. *(Introduction.)* [ev-rohland2022-3]
4. The "1240k reagent" targets slightly fewer than 1.24 million SNPs; data first appeared in 2015, and by October 2022 it had been used for more than 7,000 individuals in more than 90 papers, over 70% of genome-wide ancient human data sets. In-solution enrichment makes samples with little human DNA economical to study. *(Abstract; Introduction; Methods.)* [ev-rohland2022-4]
5. The 1240k reagent enriches targets unevenly and favours some alleles over others, complicating co-analysis with shotgun data; the Twist Ancient DNA assay (2021) gave the highest coverage, greatest uniformity and least allelic bias, and the authors' laboratory switched to it in 2021. *(Abstract; Introduction; Discussion.)* [ev-rohland2022-5]
6. Processing: read pairs merged (at least 15 bp overlap), mapped to hg19 (described as the de facto standard in ancient DNA), sequences under 30 bp dropped, PCR duplicates removed, and one random sequence chosen at each position for genotype calls. *(Methods: Data processing.)* [ev-rohland2022-6]
