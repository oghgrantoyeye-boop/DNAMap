# Allentoft et al. 2024 — population genomics of post-glacial western Eurasia

- **Citation:** Allentoft ME, Sikora M, Refoyo-Martínez A, et al. (2024) Population genomics of post-glacial western Eurasia. *Nature* 625:301–311.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-023-06865-0 · PMC10781627 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 317 new shotgun genomes, mostly Mesolithic and Neolithic, imputed with published data (>1,600 individuals). *(Abstract.)*
2. A "great divide" genetic boundary from the Black Sea to the Baltic: hunter-gatherers were strongly differentiated east and west of it, and neolithization differed: large ancestry shifts (often near-total replacement) west of it, no substantial shifts east of it in the same period. *(Abstract.)*
3. The boundary dissolved when Yamnaya-related ancestry spread ~5,000 BP, a second major turnover reaching most of Europe within ~1,000 years. *(Abstract.)*
4. Hunter-gatherers from the Middle Don contributed ancestry to Yamnaya-associated groups; Yamnaya-related groups later admixed with Globular Amphora-associated people before expanding into Europe. *(Abstract.)*
