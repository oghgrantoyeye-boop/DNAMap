# McColl et al. 2018 — prehistoric peopling of Southeast Asia

- **Citation:** McColl H, Racimo F, Vinner L, et al. (2018) The prehistoric peopling of Southeast Asia. *Science* 361:88–92.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aat3628 · **Verification:** abstract_only

## Claims we use (abstract only)
1. 26 ancient genomes (25 from Southeast Asia, 1 Jōmon). Both Hòabìnhian hunter-gatherer-related and East Asian farmer-related ancestry contributed to present-day Southeast Asian diversity, with further migrations affecting island Southeast Asia and Vietnam; neither a purely indigenous nor a simple two-layer model fits.
