# Browning et al. 2018 — two pulses of Denisovan admixture

- **Citation:** Browning SR, Browning BL, Zhou Y, Tucci S, Akey JM. (2018) Analysis of human sequence data reveals two pulses of archaic Denisovan admixture. *Cell* 173:53–61.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cell.2018.02.031 · PMC5866234 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Using a reference-free method on present-day genomes, Papuans carry substantial Denisovan-introgressed segments. *(Results.)*
2. In Japanese and Chinese (Dai, Beijing, Southern Han) populations, Denisovan segments split into two components with different affinity to the Altai Denisovan genome (~80% vs ~50% match), indicating at least two distinct Denisovan-related sources; Papuans carry mainly the more distant one. *(Section "Two waves of Denisovan ancestry"; Fig. 4; Table 2.)*

## Notes
- Present-day data; no dates for the pulses. Use for "multiple Denisovan-related sources" claim only, with `medium` confidence.
