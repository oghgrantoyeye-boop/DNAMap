# Prüfer et al. 2014 — high-coverage Altai Neandertal genome

- **Citation:** Prüfer K, Racimo F, Patterson N, et al. (2014) The complete genome sequence of a Neanderthal from the Altai Mountains. *Nature* 505:43–49.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/nature12886 · PMC4031459 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Estimated Neandertal-derived DNA in people outside Africa: 1.5–2.1%. *(Main text, "Gene flow" section; SI 14; Extended Data Table 1.)*
2. The Neandertal ancestry in non-Africans is more closely related to the Mezmaiskaya (Caucasus) Neandertal than to the Altai Neandertal — i.e. the introgressing population was not the Altai population. *(Same section; Extended Data Table 2.)*
3. Gene flow from Neandertals into Denisovans contributed at least ~0.5% of the Denisovan genome. *(Section on Denisovan heterozygosity; Fig. 6.)*
4. Denisovan-related ancestry is present in Oceanians and, at lower levels, detectable in mainland Asians and Native Americans via HMM segments. *(HMM section.)*

## Notes
- Prüfer 2017 revises the proportion upward (1.8–2.6%); both estimates are stored as alternative models.
