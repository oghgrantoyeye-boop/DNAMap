# Papac et al. 2021 — third-millennium BCE central Europe (Bohemia)

- **Citation:** Papac L, Ernée M, Dobeš M, et al. (2021) Dynamic changes in genomic and social structures in third millennium BCE central Europe. *Science Advances* 7:eabi6941.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/sciadv.abi6941 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
Read in full text on 2026-10-08 while drafting population overviews (see `research/notes/overview-brief.md`). Evidence ids `ev-papac2021-st1` … `st9` in `data/curated/evidence.json` carry the wording and the section or figure for each:

1. Globular Amphora individuals from Bohemia carry the most hunter-gatherer-related ancestry (about 26%) among the pre-Corded Ware groups there; admixture dates suggest a non-local origin. *(Results, Bohemia before Corded Ware; Fig. 3B.)*
2. Individuals from Rivnac and Globular Amphora contexts who lived when Corded Ware appeared show no detectable steppe ancestry. *(Results, Bohemia before Corded Ware.)*
3. The earliest Corded Ware individuals in Bohemia date to about 3010–2889 BCE and are genetically diverse, including females with no steppe ancestry. *(Results, Corded Ware.)*
4. A single Yamnaya source plus a central European Neolithic source fits early Corded Ware poorly; a small extra component (about 5–15%) of north-eastern hunter-gatherer-related ancestry improves the fit. *(Results, Corded Ware; Supplementary Table 17.)*
5. Y-chromosome diversity falls from five lineages in early Corded Ware to one dominant lineage (R1a-M417) in late Corded Ware, implying a male-biased process. *(Results, Corded Ware; Fig. 4A.)*
6. The authors' interpretation of early Corded Ware as a plural society (confidence low: interpretation, not measurement). *(Discussion.)*
7. Late Bell Beaker individuals carry about 20% more local Middle Eneolithic-like ancestry than early Bell Beaker individuals, who resemble Corded Ware. *(Results, Bell Beaker.)*
8. Early Bronze Age Únětice ancestry changes again; models differ (two-source Beaker model versus a north-eastern source). *(Results, EBA Únětice.)* Confidence low: alternative models.
9. Bell Beaker males mostly carry R1b-P312, not found in Corded Ware or Yamnaya males in this study. *(Introduction.)*

## Notes
- Do not turn the "plural society" reading (6) into a statement about identity or social organisation beyond what the authors say.
