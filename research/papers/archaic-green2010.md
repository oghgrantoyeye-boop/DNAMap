# Green et al. 2010 — draft Neandertal genome

- **Citation:** Green RE, Krause J, Briggs AW, et al. (2010) A draft sequence of the Neandertal genome. *Science* 328:710–722.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.1188021 · PMC5100745 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use (paraphrased)
1. Neandertals share more derived variants with present-day people outside sub-Saharan Africa than with sub-Saharan Africans, which the authors interpret as gene flow from Neandertals into the ancestors of non-Africans before Eurasian populations diverged from each other. *(Abstract; main text, "Gene flow" sections.)*
2. Their first estimate of the Neandertal-derived fraction of Eurasian genomes was 1–4%, under an assumed gene-flow date of 50–80 ka. *(Main text near the end of the admixture analysis; SOM Text 19.)*

## Notes
- Superseded numerically by Prüfer 2014/2017; kept as the original evidence that the signal exists.
- The paper itself notes that detecting Neandertal→modern gene flow does not exclude other gene-flow directions.
