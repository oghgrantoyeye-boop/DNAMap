# Harney et al. 2018 — Chalcolithic Peqi'in Cave, Israel

- **Citation:** Harney É, May H, Shalem D, et al. (2018) Ancient DNA from Chalcolithic Israel reveals the role of population mixture in cultural transformation. *Nature Communications* 9:3336.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-018-05649-9 · PMC6102297 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 22 individuals from Peqi'in Cave (Late Chalcolithic southern Levant, 4500–3900/3800 BCE) form a homogeneous population modelled as ~57% Levant Neolithic-related, ~17% Iran Chalcolithic-related, ~26% Anatolian Neolithic-related. *(Abstract.)*
