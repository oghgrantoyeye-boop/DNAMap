# Wang et al. 2019 — 3000 years in the Caucasus

- **Citation:** Wang CC, Reinhold S, Kalmykov A, et al. (2019) Ancient human genome-wide data from a 3000-year interval in the Caucasus corresponds with eco-geographic regions. *Nature Communications* 10:590.
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-018-08220-8 · **Verification:** full_text (open access)

## Claims we use
1. 45 individuals from the North Caucasus. Genetic separation between groups in the Caucasus mountains/foothills and those of the adjacent steppe. *(Abstract.)*
2. Yamnaya and later steppe pastoralist groups show farmer-related ancestry from different contact zones. *(Abstract.)*
3. "Steppe ancestry" is commonly described as a mixture of EHG- and CHG-related ancestry. *(Abstract, as framing.)*
