# Gallego Llorente et al. 2015 — Mota (Ethiopia) — WITH ERRATUM

- **Citation:** Gallego Llorente M, Jones ER, Eriksson A, et al. (2015) Ancient Ethiopian genome reveals extensive Eurasian admixture in Eastern Africa. *Science* 350:820–822. **Erratum:** *Science* (2016), doi:10.1126/science.aaf3945.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aad2879 · **Verification:** abstract_only + erratum abstract

## Status: partially retracted claim — DO NOT USE the backflow-extent claim
- The original abstract stated that Eurasian backflow reached western, central and southern Africa (6–7% in Yoruba and Mbuti).
- The 2016 erratum reports a bioinformatics error (homozygous-reference positions dropped when merging Mota with the reference panel); after correction there is **no detectable western Eurasian component in Yoruba and Mbuti**, and the paper was retitled to "...in Eastern Africa".

## Claims we use
1. Mota: ~4,500-year-old individual from Ethiopia, high-coverage genome; predates the Eurasian-related admixture seen in many present-day eastern Africans (useful as an unadmixed eastern African reference). *(Abstract, as corrected.)*

## Lesson
- Logged in LOG.md: this is why every claim is checked against the current version of a paper, including errata.
