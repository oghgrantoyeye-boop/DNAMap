# Yang et al. 2020 — northern and southern China

- **Citation:** Yang MA, Fan X, Sun B, et al. (2020) Ancient DNA indicates human population shifts and admixture in northern and southern China. *Science* 369:282–288.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aba0909 · **Verification:** abstract_only

## Claims we use (abstract only)
1. 26 individuals from northern and southern East Asia, 9500–300 BP. Genetic differentiation was higher in the past; after the Neolithic, northern East Asian ancestry spread across southern East Asia.
2. Neolithic mainland southern East Asian and Taiwan Strait island individuals show connections with Austronesian-related ancestry.
