# Skoglund et al. 2017 — prehistoric African population structure

- **Citation:** Skoglund P, Thompson JC, Prendergast ME, et al. (2017) Reconstructing prehistoric African population structure. *Cell* 171:59–71.
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cell.2017.08.049 · PMC5679310 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. 16 prehistoric Africans. The deeply divergent lineage forming the main ancestry of southern African San was more widespread: ~2/3 of the ancestry of Malawi hunter-gatherers (~8100–2500 BP) and ~1/3 of Tanzanian hunter-gatherers (~1400 BP). *(Summary.)*
2. The spread of farmers from western Africa involved complete replacement of local hunter-gatherers in some regions. *(Summary.)*
3. A ~3100 BP pastoralist from Tanzania (Luxmanda, Savanna Pastoral Neolithic) is modelled with 38 ± 1% ancestry related to Levantine Pre-Pottery Neolithic farmers (Iran/Anatolia-related sources excluded); the authors note this could reflect migration from the Levant **or** a common ancestral population earlier in Africa or the Near East. *(Results.)*
4. Admixture between western- and eastern-African-related lineages in some Kenyan groups dated ~800–400 years ago (LD-based). *(Results.)*
