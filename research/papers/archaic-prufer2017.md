# Prüfer et al. 2017 — high-coverage Vindija Neandertal genome

- **Citation:** Prüfer K, de Filippo C, Grote S, et al. (2017) A high-coverage Neandertal genome from Vindija Cave in Croatia. *Science* 358:655–658.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.aao1887 · PMC6185897 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Vindija 33.19 (Croatia; radiocarbon >45.5 ka BP, ~50 ka by genetic estimate) and Mezmaiskaya 1 share more alleles with present-day non-Africans than the Altai Neandertal does: the introgressing Neandertals were closer to these western Neandertals. *(Main text.)*
2. Revised estimate: non-Africans outside Oceania carry 1.8–2.6% Neandertal DNA; East Asians 2.3–2.6%, western Eurasians 1.8–2.4%. *(Main text; Fig. 4A.)*
