# Schubert et al. 2016 — AdapterRemoval v2: trimming adapters and merging reads

- **Citation:** Schubert M, Lindgreen S, Orlando L. (2016) AdapterRemoval v2: rapid adapter trimming, identification, and read merging. *BMC Research Notes* 9:88.
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s13104-016-1900-2 · PMC4751634 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. When short DNA molecules such as those from fossils are sequenced, reads run into the adapter sequences, which must be trimmed; overlapping read pairs can be merged to reconstruct the whole original molecule with better accuracy, which is particularly useful for ancient DNA. *(Abstract; Findings: Background.)* [ev-schubert2016-1]
