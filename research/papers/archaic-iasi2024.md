# Iasi et al. 2024 — Neandertal ancestry through time

- **Citation:** Iasi LNM, Chintalapati M, Skov L, et al. (2024) Neandertal ancestry through time: insights from genomes of ancient and present-day humans. *Science* 386:eadq3010.
- **DOI:** 10.1126/science.adq3010 · PMC12184710 · **Verification:** full_text

## Claims we use
1. Most Neandertal ancestry in modern humans is attributable to one shared, extended period of gene flow, dated 50,500–43,500 years ago, inferred from >300 ancient and present-day genomes. *(Abstract; Discussion.)*
2. Because this gene flow is shared by most non-Africans, its timing gives a lower bound on when the ancestors of non-Africans had left Africa and were still one population. *(Discussion.)*
