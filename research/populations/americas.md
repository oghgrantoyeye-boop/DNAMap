## Founding population of Native Americans (inferred) (`first-americans-founders`)

*genetic cluster · confidence medium · Northeast Asia / Beringia (not located by genetics)*

A modelled population: the shared ancestors of Ancient Beringians and all other Native Americans, which separated from East Asian-related populations, received gene flow related to Ancient North Eurasians, and later branched. Its location during this time is not known from genetics.

**Membership:** inferred only (no sampled individuals).

**Evidence:**
- Demographic model: split from East Asians ~36 ± 1.5 ka with gene flow until ~25 ka; ANE-related gene flow 25–20 ka; Ancient Beringians branched 22–18.1 ka; northern and southern Native American branches diverged 17.5–14.6 ka. — morenomayar2018a, Abstract
- The ancestors of all present-day Native Americans entered the Americas from Siberia as a single migration wave no earlier than 23,000 years ago, after at most ~8,000 years of isolation in Beringia. — raghavan2015, Abstract
- An estimated 14–38% of Native American ancestry may derive from gene flow from a population related to MA-1, after divergence from East Asian ancestors and before Native American diversification. — raghavan2014, Abstract
- Native Americans diversified into two basal branches around 13,000 years ago. — raghavan2015, Abstract

**Caveats:**
- The window spans the modelled split from East Asians (~36,000 years ago) to the divergence of northern and southern branches (~14,600 years ago, lower bound) in Moreno-Mayar 2018 (abstract only); other studies place the founding migration later (no earlier than 23,000 years ago, Raghavan 2015).
