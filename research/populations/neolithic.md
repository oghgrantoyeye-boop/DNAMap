## Neolithic farmers of Britain (`britain-neolithic`)

*archaeological · confidence high · Britain*

Farming communities of Britain from about 4000 BCE, whose ancestry derives mainly from continental farmers, with small, regionally varying contributions from local hunter-gatherers.

**Membership rule:** `^(England_EN(_Megalithic)?|England_N|Scotland_N|Wales_N)$`.

**Matched (114 individuals, 46 sites):** Scotland_N (44); England_EN_Megalithic (32); England_N (29); Wales_N (6); England_EN (3)

**Evidence:**
- Farming came to Britain with continental farmers whose ancestry was mostly Anatolian-farmer-related (about 74% on average in the authors' model), with small hunter-gatherer contributions and no later hunter-gatherer resurgence. — brace2019, Abstract; Results
- Continental farmers are modelled as arriving in Britain by 3975–3722 cal BCE (95%). — brace2019, Results
