## Early European founder population (Kostenki/Sunghir-related) (`early-european-founders`)

*genetic cluster · confidence medium · Eastern European Plain*

Individuals from western Russia, about 38,000–32,000 years ago, who belong to the founder population that contributed to all later sampled European hunter-gatherers until about 14,000 years ago.

**Membership rule:** `^Russia_Kostenki_UP$|^Russia_Sunghir_UP$`.

*Kostenki 14 is the type individual in Fu 2016; Sunghir is grouped with it as related to the Věstonice cluster in Posth 2023.*

**Matched (6 individuals, 2 sites):** Russia_Sunghir_UP (4); Russia_Kostenki_UP (2)

**Evidence:**
- From about 37,000 years ago (Kostenki 14) to about 14,000 years ago, all sampled Europeans descend from one founder population that is part of present-day European ancestry; earlier individuals such as Oase 1 did not contribute substantially. — fu2016, Abstract; clustering section

**Caveats:**
- Grouping Kostenki and Sunghir together is our simplification; both papers treat them as related members of the early European founder lineage.
