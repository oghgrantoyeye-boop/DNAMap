## Zlatý kůň/Ranis population (`zlatykun-ranis`)

*genetic cluster · confidence high · Central Europe (Germany, Czechia)*

A small population of early modern humans known from two sites in central Europe, about 45,000 years ago. The individuals were closely related to each other, even across sites.

**Genetic profile.** The earliest split from the out-of-Africa population sampled so far. No detectable continuity with later Europeans.

**Archaeological context.** The Ranis remains are associated with the Lincombian–Ranisian–Jerzmanowician (LRJ) assemblage.

- The Neandertal ancestry of the Zlatý kůň/Ranis population comes from the admixture shared by all non-Africans, dated to 45,000–49,000 years ago. — sumer2024, Main text, dating section
