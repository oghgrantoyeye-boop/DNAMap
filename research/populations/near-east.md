## Natufian-associated hunter-gatherers (`natufians`)

*archaeological · confidence high · Southern Levant*

Hunter-gatherers of the southern Levant associated with the Natufian culture, before the beginning of farming. About half their ancestry is modelled as coming from a 'Basal Eurasian' lineage.

**Archaeological context.** Natufian.

**Membership rule:** `^Israel_Natufian$`.

**Caveats:**

## Basal Eurasian lineage (modelled) (`basal-eurasian`)

*genetic cluster · confidence medium · Unknown*

A modelled ancestral lineage that split from other non-African lineages before they diversified. It is needed to fit the ancestry of early Near Easterners and Early European Farmers, but no individual from it has been sampled.

**Membership:** inferred only (no sampled individuals).

**Evidence:**
- Early European Farmers are modelled with about 44% ancestry from a 'Basal Eurasian' lineage that split before other non-African lineages diversified. — lazaridis2014, Abstract; admixture-graph section
- The earliest sampled Near Eastern populations derived about half their ancestry from a 'Basal Eurasian' lineage with little or no Neandertal ancestry. — lazaridis2016, Abstract

**Caveats:**
- The time range is a display window only: the papers place the lineage's split before the diversification of other non-Africans and its contribution before the earliest sampled Natufians, without dating it precisely.

**Archaeological context.** Late Chalcolithic of the southern Levant.

**Membership rule:** `^Israel_LateC$`.
