## Indus Periphery Cline and the Rakhigarhi individual (`indus-periphery`)

*genetic cluster · confidence medium · Northwest South Asia and its periphery*

A genetic profile shared by one individual from an Indus Valley Civilization context (Rakhigarhi) and by outliers buried at sites in Iran and Turkmenistan that were in contact with the IVC: a mixture of ancient-Iranian-related and South Asian hunter-gatherer-related ancestry, with little or no steppe-related ancestry.

**Membership rule:** `^India_Harappan$`.

*AADR v66.p1 does not label the 11 Indus Periphery outliers separately (they sit inside numbered clusters such as Turkmenistan_BA1-1 and Iran_ShahriSokhta_BA2-2); adding them needs the individual list from Narasimhan 2019.*

**Matched (1 individuals, 1 sites):** India_Harappan (1)

**Evidence:**
- A genome from an IVC context (Rakhigarhi) fits as a mixture of ancient-Iranian-related (largest) and Southeast Asian hunter-gatherer-related ancestry, matching the Indus Periphery outliers, with little or no steppe-related ancestry. — shinde2019, Summary
- Eleven outlier individuals at Gonur and Shahr-i-Sokhta form an 'Indus Periphery Cline'; equating it with the Indus Valley Civilization population is a hypothesis with circumstantial support. — narasimhan2019, Main text

**Caveats:**
- Only one genome comes from an IVC context; whether it represents the IVC population as a whole is a hypothesis.
- Membership is incomplete (see notes).

## Ancestral Ancient South Asians (AASI, modelled) (`aasi`)

*genetic cluster · confidence medium · South Asia*

A modelled ancestry component: the South Asian lineage with no relationship to West Eurasians, split around the time East Asian, Andamanese and Australian ancestors separated. No unmixed individual has been sampled.

**Aliases:** AASI (narasimhan2019)

**Membership:** inferred only (no sampled individuals).

**Evidence:**
- AASI ('Ancestral Ancient South Asians') is modelled as an Asian lineage that split around the time East Asian, Andamanese and Australian ancestors separated. — narasimhan2019, Admixture-graph section; Fig. 5
- The main source of ancestry in South Asians is a prehistoric gradient between people related to early Iranian hunter-gatherers and people related to South/Southeast Asian hunter-gatherers (AASI). — narasimhan2019, Abstract; Fig. 5

**Caveats:**
- The time range is a display window, not a dated lifespan.

## Ancestral South Indians (ASI, modelled) (`asi`)

*genetic cluster · confidence medium · South Asia*

A modelled ancestral population that formed after the decline of the Indus Valley Civilization as Indus Periphery-related people mixed with groups further southeast. It is one of the two main sources of ancestry in present-day South Asians.

**Aliases:** ASI (narasimhan2019)

**Membership:** inferred only (no sampled individuals).

**Evidence:**
- After the IVC's decline, Indus Periphery-related people mixed with south-eastern groups to form Ancestral South Indians (ASI), and with steppe-pastoralist-descended people arriving via Central Asia after ~4000 years ago to form Ancestral North Indians (ANI). — narasimhan2019, Abstract; main text

**Caveats:**
- Display window; formation date is model-based.
