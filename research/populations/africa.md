## Eastern African foragers (`east-african-foragers`)

*genetic cluster · confidence medium · Eastern Africa (Kenya, Tanzania)*

Hunter-gatherers of eastern Africa over the last 18,000 years, carrying a deep eastern African lineage. Their ancestry later mixed with incoming herders and farmers.

**Membership rule:** `^Kenya_(LSA|Kansyore|LSA_Kansyore)$|^Tanzania_(LSA|LSA_Kisese)$`.

**Evidence:**
